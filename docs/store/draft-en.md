## About the game — 990 characters (100–1000)

The riverside village stands empty, and only you can bring it back. The current carries logs, flowers, stones and fish — catch them and pair them up to make them grow: a wood chip turns into a boat, a seed into a flower crown, a grain of sand into a mill wheel, a fry into a goldfish.

Villagers wait on the piers: the fisherman, the carpenter, the miller, the gardener, the bathhouse keeper and the bridge builder. Fill their orders, earn coins and stars, and restore the pier, the shed, the mill, the flower garden, the bathhouse and the bridge. Every building helps: new chapters, more room in the backwater, gifts and extra coins.

There are 36 levels in three chapters, four chains of six steps and four helpers: the Dam, the Hook, the Magic drop and the Net. Once the pier is restored, the River of the Day opens: a new level every day, the same for everyone, with its own leaderboard. No hurry? Switch on the Calm river and the current flows 1.5 times slower. Progress saves at once.

## How to play — 987 characters (100–1000)

The river flows down, moving the items one cell every few seconds. Join two identical items to make one of the next level; quick merges in a row build a combo and give more coins. A new item merges by itself with a matching neighbour — a cascade.

The green cells along the edges are the bank: nothing flows there, so keep what you need for later. Items that reach the bottom drop into the backwater and can be picked up again. When it is full, an item floats away and a heart breaks; with all hearts broken, the level is lost. A boulder blocks its lane; a snag disappears when you merge something next to it.

Villagers on the piers wait for orders: bring what they ask for. The level is complete when every order is filled.

On a phone: drag an item with your finger onto another item, the bank, a pier or the backwater, or tap an item, then its target.

On a computer: the same with the mouse — drag an item, or click it, then the target. Escape or P pauses, keys 1–4 use the helpers.
